The tutorial takes about a minute and teaches running, jumping, diving and every obstacle you will meet. We recommend it before your first show.

The show

Pick 3, 4 or 5 rounds. Each show picks random rounds: a race to open, then random races and survival rounds, and one of three finals. Players who don't qualify are knocked out. Last one standing in the final wins the crown.

Multiplayer

Press Multiplayer, create a room and share its 4-letter code. Up to 20 people can join with the same code. Fewer qualify each round until one player takes the crown. Knocked out? Keep watching with ←→.

Rounds

  • Races (9): reach the finish before the spots fill up. Round 1 is always a race.
  • Survival (Tile Panic, Meteor Meadow, Blizzard Bluff): stay on until enough players fall.
  • Finals (Spin Arena, Cannon Clash, Pinball Pit): last one standing wins the crown.

Tips

  • Jump just before a spinning bar reaches you.
  • Jump, then dive in mid-air to clear big gaps.
  • Falling in a race sends you back to the last checkpoint.
