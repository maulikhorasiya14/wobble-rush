WobbleRush

Run. Jump. Wobble. Win the crown.

First time here?

The tutorial takes about a minute and teaches running, jumping, diving and every obstacle you will meet. We recommend it before your first show.

How to play

Controls

WASD / arrowsMove
SpaceJump
Shift or EDive (works in mid-air too)
Esc or PPause

The show

You and 11 computer players compete over 3 rounds. Players who don't qualify are knocked out. Last one standing in the final wins the crown.

Rounds

  1. Spin Sprint (race): first 8 over the finish line qualify.
  2. Tile Panic (survival): tiles crumble after you touch them. Last 4 standing qualify.
  3. Spin Arena (final): jump the sweeping beam and stay on the shrinking arena.

Tips

  • Jump just before a spinning bar reaches you.
  • Jump, then dive in mid-air to clear big gaps.
  • Falling in a race sends you back to the last checkpoint.

The full guide is in GUIDE.md in the project folder.
